Support

Noa gives you one small thing to learn each day: a question, a short lesson, and a few questions to make it stick. About five minutes, then you're done.

Get in touch

Email contactnoalearning@gmail.com about anything: a bug, a fact in a lesson that looks wrong, or an idea. Please include the topic and lesson name if it's about a lesson.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. There is nothing to sign up for, and Noa works with no signal.

I missed a day. Is my streak gone? Probably not. You get two streak freezes a month, and a freeze covers a missed day automatically. If a streak does end, nothing you've learned goes with it: finished lessons, XP and ranks all stay.

How do I turn off reminders, sounds or vibration? Open Settings inside the app. Daily reminder, Sounds and Haptics each have their own switch.

Where is my progress stored? On your phone. Noa never uploads your answers or progress to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Does Quick practice count? It earns up to 25 XP a day, but it never affects your streak. The daily lesson is what keeps a streak going.

A lesson says something wrong. Please tell us. Every question links to the source it came from, and a correction is the most useful thing you can send.
