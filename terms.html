Accuracy

Noa's curated catalog is drafted with the help of an AI model and checked by a person before it ships, and every question carries a link to its source so you can check it yourself.

Custom topics are generated by an AI model. They are drafted quickly and are not reviewed by a person before you see them. They can be wrong. Do not rely on Noa for medical, legal, financial, or safety decisions. If a question looks wrong, report it from the app so it can be corrected.

Noa is educational material, not professional advice.
